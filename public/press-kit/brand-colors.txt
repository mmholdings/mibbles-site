MIBBLES BRAND COLORS
================================================================

PRIMARY
-------
Cream         #FAFAF7   (background)
Ink           #1A1A1A   (primary text)
Terracotta    #E27D5F   (accent)

EXTENDED PALETTE
----------------
Cream-200     #F2F1EC   (subtle section backgrounds)
Ink-700       #2A2A2A   (secondary text)
Ink-500       #5C5C5C   (tertiary text)
Terracotta-50 #FBF1ED   (light accent backgrounds)
Terracotta-700 #A24A2C  (active accent text)

USAGE
-----
- Body text always on cream or cream-200.
- Terracotta is for accents, eyebrows, and small CTAs. Never for body type.
- The ink-900 background is for "dark mode" sections (pricing CTA, footer-adjacent).
